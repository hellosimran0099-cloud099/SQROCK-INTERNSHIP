6. Analysis
The scan verified that TCP port 8080 is open on the local development host. An open port indicates that a local service is accepting TCP connections. The port number alone does not definitively identify the application, so the associated process should be identified through local system inspection.
7. Security Consideration
Developer environments should expose only services that are required. Unnecessary network-accessible development or administration services can increase the attack surface. Access controls, firewall rules, authentication, and appropriate network segmentation should be applied where required.
8. Conclusion
The Day 17 practical successfully verified local TCP socket exposure. Among the five tested ports, port 8080 was detected as open on 127.0.0.1. The result provides a basic network exposure map for the local developer environment and identifies port 8080 for further service/process verification.
